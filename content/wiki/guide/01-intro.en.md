Elysia keeps the workflow simple: Zola reads Markdown from `content/`, templates render it as blog, Wiki, or standalone pages. The full feature and directory example live in the pinned post *Hello, Elysia*; this chapter only explains how to use the guide.

## Suitable and not suitable

- Suitable: text-focused blogs, maintained docs / Wiki, sites that need categories and archives.
- Not suitable: card streams that require cover images, or heavy Headless CMS integration (requires custom extension).

| Chapter | Topic | Outcome |

| 01 | Meet Elysia (this page) | Decide fit and reading path |

## How to read

- New users: follow 01 → 02 → 03, preview locally, then read 04 and 05 as needed.
- Existing Zola site: add the theme as a submodule, then jump to 03 and 05.
- Just writing: go to 05 for Front Matter and components.
